# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

The Attack and Defense Playbook for Microsoft Entra is a community-driven, defensive-security publication. We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone who participates, regardless of experience, identity, background, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, professional, and constructive.
- Discuss attack and defense techniques responsibly, with an emphasis on authorized testing, detection, mitigation, and education.
- Base technical feedback on evidence, reproducible observations, and the goals of the playbook.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality. Never post credentials, access tokens, tenant data, customer data, personal data, or other sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private, confidential, or sensitive information without permission.
- Content that facilitates unauthorized access, abuse, or harm.
- Behavior that makes participation unsafe, hostile, or unwelcoming.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject content, and may limit participation when this Code of Conduct is violated. To report a concern privately, contact a repository maintainer through GitHub with “Code of Conduct” in the subject or opening message. Do not use public issues or pull requests for sensitive reports.

This Code of Conduct applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces representing the Attack and Defense Playbook for Microsoft Entra community.
